Sample Mountain Trek Plan

Your perfect high-altitude adventure, step by step

Day 1
Arrival at Base Camp
Reach the hill station, meet your trek guide and fellow adventurers. Acclimatization walk and gear check. Bonfire dinner under the stars.
Day 2
First Summit Attempt
Early 5 AM start. Trek through dense forests, meadows and rocky terrain to reach the first viewpoint. Packed breakfast with mountain views.
Day 3
High Camp & Stargazing
Reach high camp at 13,000 ft. Afternoon free time for photography. Evening stargazing session — the Milky Way like you've never seen it.
Day 4
Summit Day
Push for the peak at dawn. Standing at the top with the world below you — the feeling is indescribable. Descent and celebration dinner.
Day 5
Return & Memories
Trek back to base, hot shower and certificate of achievement. Stories that will last a lifetime.
